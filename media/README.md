# Media

`bbb-stutter.mp4` is the first 20 seconds of Big Buck Bunny, re-encoded as
H.264 and AAC in MP4. The suite plays its first 10 s with `--clips bbb`.

https://peach.blender.org/, licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution
3.0 licence (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). This copy is cut
to 20 seconds and re-encoded.
